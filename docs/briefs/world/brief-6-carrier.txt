RULES, each learned from a pass that went wrong:
- The file headers are the specification: keep them and update them with what you did.
- No GLSL uniform starting with `at` (injected by installFog into your materials); yours start with `cv`.
- Materials and geometries are never cached module-wide; textures may be.
- Name every material ('carrier/deck', 'carrier/island' ...); onBeforeCompile materials carry a versioned customProgramCacheKey.
- No Math.random(): makeRng/noise2/fbm2 from ../config.js, seeded from the hull number or the ship's length.
- Keep every exported name and return shape: buildDeck, buildHull, buildGallery, buildIsland -> { group, radar }, buildWires -> Object3D[], buildLensHousing(c, lensPos) -> Object3D, buildWake -> Mesh (carrier.js sets wake.material.opacity), and every PALETTE.deck / PALETTE.carrier key that exists now (the LightSet colours and sizes are read by carrier.js).
